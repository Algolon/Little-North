# LN-FOX-R01b — focused head and chest form development

Continuation authorized after R01a ART REJECT. Work remains on `art/ln-fox-r01a-character-study`; R01a assets are preserved and R01b outputs have a separate directory. The base is the actual R01a `.blend`, not a rebuilt proxy.

## Design decisions

- Expression: alert and gentle. Almond eye apertures, recessed orbital structure, substantial upper lids; avoid exposed spherical bead eyes, angry slanted brows, and human smiles.
- Muzzle: compact fox wedge; a rounded triangular nose and an incised mouth line must clarify the muzzle without enlarging it into a canine snout.
- Cheek-to-neck: broad swept cheek planes continue into the chest coat envelope. Avoid pendulous round cheek bags or disconnected leaf tufts.
- Ruff: a small set of broad, rooted flow masses establish directional structure. These are macro volumes for neutral-clay assessment, not a completed fur treatment.
- Ears: shallow conchal bowls with a thick perimeter; keep the compact arctic proportions.

## Method change

The previous script's global proportion tweaking did not reach the quality target. R01b edits the preserved anatomical mesh locally. The initial attempt used Boolean recesses, lid geometry and rooted ruff lofts; actual renders exposed block-like tabs and lid seams. The correction replaces those additions with continuous-skin sculpt fields, orbital tissue deformation, beveled almond apertures and a resurfaced head/chest mesh. This is actual Blender mesh editing executed on a CPU runner; it is not claimed to be a manual sculpt-brush session.

## Evidence and decision order

Render head front, head side and head three-quarter close-ups, plus front/side/rear/three-quarter of the full character. Compare the face at both scales. Inspect the eyes for correct seating, readable lids and socket continuity; inspect chest for lobes that resemble glued-on primitives. Do not equate Boolean union or a valid GLB with sculptural coherence. No premium painterly coat pass until the relevant form gate is convincing.

## Remaining original limitations

R01a's hocks, paws, tail coat and actual gameplay-camera calibration remain unresolved. Head/chest improvement alone cannot pass the overall hero ART ACCEPT gate. No rig, animation, production integration, merge or deployment.
