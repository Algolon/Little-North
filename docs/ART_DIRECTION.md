# Little North — Visual Art Direction v0.1

## Sculpted Arctic Storybook

A handcrafted, stylized Arctic world that feels like a beautiful sculpted storybook brought to life. Soft, rounded snowdrifts cover deliberately shaped, faceted stone. Asymmetric spruce trees carry substantial, irregular snow mantles. Warm wood and amber window glow form a refuge against the immense, quiet northern world.

**Emotional promise**: cozy wonder, quiet courage, winter magic. Scale contrast: tiny Arctic fox against surprisingly vast nature. Never harsh survival, horror, glossy realism, or generic toy-world repetition.

**Form grammar**
- Snow: broad, quiet curves with gentle layered wind ridges; never an entirely flat white plane.
- Rock: sculpted faceted planes with beveled proportions and credible snow deposition.
- Spruce: unique organic tier silhouettes with hanging needles and snow caps; **not** stacked cones.
- Cabin: solid structural roof/walls, tactile log rhythm, restrained detail and warm windows.
- Fox: recognizable quadrupedal arctic fox, oversized fluffy tail, short legs, compact muzzle; personality comes from posing, not clothes.

**Material grammar**
- Snow: blue-tinted white, high roughness, diffuse sunlight, restrained subsurface impression.
- Pine: blue-deep-green foliage with lighter frost edges.
- Rock: slate-blue, subtle faceting, locally held snow.
- Timber: desaturated warm umber, low specular.
- Ice: turquoise transparency in special focal locations (not every surface).

**Lighting**: cool cyan atmospheric fill with slightly warm low winter sun; use clear direct/ambient contrast rather than blanket high bloom. Home = amber warmth; woods = soft blue-green stillness; summit = peach/pink horizon above the clouds.

**Creative sources**: user-supplied moodboard (10-19-2026), including visually stylized snowy woods, crystalline ice, miniature Arctic geography, and mountain cloud inversion. The images are inspiration only, not distributable assets.

**Camera**: third-person, readable fox silhouette, landscape framed through trees. Designed for desktop and eventual Android mobile browser.
